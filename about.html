What's My Story?

I graduated from UC Davis with a BA in Design and Cinema & Digital Media, exploring everything from graphic design to interaction design — and developing the empathy for user experience that launched my UI/UX career. Two competition projects with Design Interactive turned that into product work: an eight-hour designathon and a three-week sprint, both award-winning, both better scoping lessons than any class.

At DigitalNest, I take client work from research through to shipped front-end — brand standards, information architecture, high-fidelity design, and everything in between.
